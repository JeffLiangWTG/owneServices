Tobacco Products Tax

The table below contains the current rates of Tobacco Products Tax.

These rates are effective from 11 October 2023.

Tobacco Products Tax
ProductRate of Duty (€)
Cigarettes (a) €428.48 per thousand together with an amount equal to 8.85% of the price at which the cigarettes are sold by retail,
or
(b) €479.37 per thousand where the rate of tax would be less than if calculated in accordance with paragraph (a)
Cigars €483.343 per kilogram
Fine-cut tobacco for the rolling of cigarettes €465.003 per kilogram
Other smoking tobacco €335.322 per kilogram
