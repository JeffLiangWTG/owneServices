The ediEnterprise Service Request module communicates with the CargoWise edi Customer Service system using the eHub Inbound/OutBound Messages service tasks. If you find that your Service Requests have not been responded in a timely manner, this could be due to these service tasks not running correctly.

Troubleshooting suggestions:

-          Ensure that the eHub Inbound Messages (EHI) service task is running correctly and its logs are not showing errors.

-          Ensure that the eHub Outbound Messages (EHO) service task is running correctly and its logs are not showing errors.

-          Ensure that the System Message Service (SYS) service task is running correctly and its logs are not showing errors.
